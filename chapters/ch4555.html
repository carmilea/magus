Tezka the Suneater flew at low altitude and speed as he tried to orient himself on the map. Being a dimensional mage was of no help when you hadn’t visited a place in millennia and everywhere you looked was the same.

"Where in Mogar’s name am I?" He turned around, finding no trace of the landmarks he was supposed to use to orient himself.

As the three great countries progressed in reclaiming and colonizing Jiera, the Organization’s operations had to move further away from the scouts to avoid detection by the members of both Awakened Councils.

Jiera’s natives resented the Garleners for claiming a few regions already as their own, but they couldn’t complain about the results. The joint efforts of the three great countries, the elves, the undead, and the people of Zelex, had greatly reduced the threat of the monster tides.

Even better, the allied forces from Garlen had also recaptured a few lost cities with the help of the Jierans. Death was no longer a daily occurrence for the survivors of the plague, and all the four races could finally thrive again.

The Beast Empire was a great supporter of the colonization efforts. The Garleners rebuilt human cities and roads, and offered the refugees new places to live.

After taking the human survivors in, the beasts had forced them to learn Tyris’ universal language, making communication easy for everyone.

The humans from Jiera didn’t like following the laws and customs of those they considered invaders much, but they preferred the Garleners’ open cities to the beasts’ underground complexes and their swift, merciless justice system.

Now that the Jieran Council didn’t live in a state of eternal crisis, the Awakened finally had time to check the abandoned magical mines and reclaim what they could before their Garlen counterparts did.

The Master wanted to avoid pointless conflicts, so he had his workforce migrate to wild and dangerous territories where the Awakened Councils wouldn’t dare venture for a long while.

The presence of the Eldritches and Auros the Bringer of Unity was more than enough to ensure the safety of the miners and the death of any bold explorer in an unfortunate "accident".

Yet the Master liked to play it safe, so he had sent Tezka to scout future sites of his mining operations.

The Suneater was tasked with culling the local monster population, studying potentially dangerous mutated monsters, and crafting the Home Stones that let the Organization relocate its miners, prospectors, and tons of equipment thousands of kilometers away in a few hours instead of months.

Such long-distance devices required a dimensional crystal and pinpoint-accurate coordinates, something only the Suneater could achieve. The task was boring and time-consuming, but it saved the Organization countless resources and was safe for someone of Tezka’s skill.

Hoping it would help the Suneater finish his mission faster, Filia and Frey had given him a full set of Rune Stores. The kids couldn’t even multi cast, and the remaining set was enough for them and their mother to train their powers.

Tezka kept the six foam constructs floating over his tails at all times, even when he split into multiple bodies to explore his surroundings and find his way. Being able to be in nine places at the same time was another reason the Suneater had been chosen as scout.

He couldn’t fly as fast as Zoreth, but he could explore a much wider area in a brief time and then return to the main base in a heartbeat with dimensional magic.
