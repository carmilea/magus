"No." Althika cried, battling the despair ravaging her heart.

She was about to die, and every egotistical instinct she had screamed for her to satisfy her hunger and run away. Yet she refused to listen, preferring to die with dignity as Althika the Dryad rather than as a monstrous Vurdalak.

"I don’t want this for anyone. Especially my baby boy. Is he suffering? Does he feel what I feel?"

"Verhen?" Althika turned to Lith. "Is there nothing you can do?"

’He’s younger and much less knowledgeable than Baba Yaga, but I won’t surrender my child’s life until there is a single shred of hope left.’ She thought.

"I think I can save you." Lith replied, shocking everyone. "I think I can save you both."

"How?" Baba Yaga asked. "This is my first time seeing something like this, and I doubt you ever encountered an illness remotely similar, let alone treated it."

"It’s my first time as well, yet I know there’s something I can do." Lith’s face was a mask of focus as he searched inside himself for the right words. "It’s an instinct similar to when I helped Solus or Ely helped Zoreth.

"You hear voices?" Naphila sneered, and the Crone squeezed her Root into a crumpled mess to shut her up.

"Are you sure?" Baba Yaga asked. "I’m not doubting you. I know who you are and what you are capable of. I just want to make sure you realize how much is at stake here. You risk destroying three lives if you are wrong.

"Althika’s, her son’s, and your own. Can you live with the consequences of your actions if you fail?"

"I don’t know." Lith shook his head. "The only thing I’m certain is that I won’t be able to live with myself if I don’t at least try. I can’t promise you anything, Althika. I can’t give you an explanation or my odds of success.

Baba Yaga used the arrays of the lair to restrain Althika before she assaulted Lith and used her tier five Body Sculpting spell, Cradle of Life, to suppress the influence of Orpal’s fragment over the Dryad’s mind and Light Soul before the corruption of the Vurdalak’s flesh spread further.

"Leave that to me." A wave of the Crone’s hand depowered most of the arrays of the Redleaf Clan, flooding Bloodhaven and Starforge with all the world energy they needed.

She and Solus conjured the magical formations of the towers to seal the medical ward from any external influence.

"Okay, Meln, this is between you and me, like usual." Lith moved to the Dryad’s bedside and took deep, slow breaths. "The only difference is that this time you can’t run away like you always do."

Then he turned to Althika.

"This may hurt a lot, and for that, I’m sorry. I need you to be brave and not struggle, no matter how scary it gets, okay?"

"Okay." She nodded, too busy holding the Light Soul under control to feel any fear.

Only then did he unfurl his six wings and use the world energy he absorbed through them to wreath himself in Dread Flames. Lith placed his right thumb in the middle of the Dryad’s forehead and his left thumb above her heart.

’Wrong!’ The Voidfeather Dragon roared. ’It’s all wrong! Wrong!’

Lith opened his mouth, exhaling a slow stream of silvery flames as thin as a hair that struck where the black mist of Orpal’s influence was thickest. Althika clenched her teeth in agony as her flesh burned, while the black mist reeled at the contact.

Lith heard Orpal scream in pain amid the distorted melody of the Dryad’s life force, but it only lasted a second. Her regenerative abilities instantly closed the wound, and the black mist moved away from the silvery flames.

Lith tried a few more times, but the result was always the same. The black mist dodged the flames by using Althika’s body as a literal meat shield and exploiting her healing factor to cover its escape.
