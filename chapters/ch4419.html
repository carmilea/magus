Ajatar also possessed the notes regarding his technique for awakening a Lesser Divine Beast’s Guardian bloodline. Still, he had already promised to share them and had no intention of going back on his word.

After the meal ended, Ajatar asked Jirni and Orion for a minute of their time. They moved to the tea room in the Ernas’ living quarters to have some privacy.

"I didn’t come here only to see Orikan and Jirya." He said after Jirni served everyone tea and oven-fresh biscuits. "I want to ask your help for a project of mine."

"What can we possibly do that the Fire Dragon clan can’t do better?" Jirni frowned.

The Ernas barely knew the Sapphire Dragon, but according to his background check, he was rich, and the Drake bloodline was ancient enough to have access to any resource Ajatar might need.

’Even a founding pillar of the Kingdom doesn’t amount to much when compared to Leegaain’s original bloodline. Drakes exist for almost as long as he does, whereas the Ernas house was founded a little less than a thousand years ago.’ She thought.

"You don’t know how to cloak your spells?" Ajatar said in surprise. "I can teach you how to do that, but first let me answer your question. You should help me to ensure the safety of your son-in-law, Morok Ernas."

Hearing their family name attached to the Tyrant still made the Ernas couple flinch.

"To ensure that no matter what happens to you or me, your daughter won’t become a widow and her children grow without their father. I’m as stingy as any other Dragon, yet I’m willing to part with some of my most prized possessions because I care about Morok and I want to ensure his safety.

His words struck Jirni and Orion like a punch, squeezing the air out of their lungs. The Sapphire Dragon was calm and composed, seeming to have put much more thought into the matter than they ever had.

’The lizard is right.’ Orion thought. ’I’ve been so caught up in my grief for my Little Flower’s death and the Gernoffs’ threat looming over Jirni’s life that I let my dislike for Morok blind me.

"I want to do my part, Ajatar." Orion replied. "Whatever you are going to use, please allow me to contribute half the materials. Nothing is worth more than my daughter’s smile."

"As you wish." The Sapphire Dragon inwardly sighed with relief. "The main reason I need your help, however, is that I have no idea how humans fight and I want to keep the secret of the evolved humans’ powers in the family.

"I don’t trust any outsider with such knowledge. It could be used not only against Morok, but also his children, Lith, and even the people of Zelex."

"You’re damn right." Orion snorted.

It contained every detail of the weapons and their abilities, but no mention of the crafting method necessary to assemble the various parts or the Forgemastering technique to combine the pseudo cores.
