"Not like a date. A date." Haug clarified.

"Yes! I’d love to!" Ryla smiled from the bottom of her heart, and her wings flapped so hard she now floated a few centimeters off the ground.

Haug should have been on cloud nine, yet he shared but a fraction of her excitement.

’Don’t delude yourself, old man. This doesn’t mean she likes you. Only that she likes the idea of having her first date.’ He thought. ’There’s no way to know if she wouldn’t react the same way even if anyone else asked her out.’

"We... I mean, I might have a problem, though." Her wings stopped moving, and her expression tensed. "I... I’m..."

"Don’t worry, Morok told me everything already." Haug noticed her distress and did his best to reassure her.

"He did?" Ryla went pale, and her wings froze in a half-unfurled stance, ready to take flight at a moment’s notice.

’I’m going to kill him!’ She thought.

"Yes." Haug replied. "He told me of your fallen state and that stepping out of the mana geyser would shorten your lifespan. You don’t have to worry about that. I’ll take care of Warping us across mana geysers until we reach our destination."

"Oh, that!" She sighed in relief, and her smile returned.

’I was actually worried about turning into a monster and Morok telling Haug about the circumstances of my... relationship with Glemos. Thank the gods he didn’t.’ She thought, and she was dead wrong.

"Also, if it makes you feel more comfortable, we could bring Garrik with us." Haug said. "I want to get to know you and-"

"No!" Ryla cut him short, her cheeks flushed and her fists clenched. "I mean, that’s not necessary. I’m comfortable around you, Haug, and..."

She struggled to let the words out and needed to take deep breaths to find the strength to say what she wanted.

"And I’d like to get to know you as Haug the man instead of Haug the bartender, just like I want you to get to know me as Ryla instead of Garrik’s mother."

"I would love to." He smiled back at her, and for the first time since the start of that conversation, he meant it. "What about Garrik? Won’t he feel lonely?"

"It’s just one evening." She shrugged. "Besides, I’m not leaving him stranded in the Blood Desert, but surrounded by his family. Give me a second."

She rushed outside, found Morok, and carried him over her head inside the Tavern.

"Haug here asked me out, and I said yes." She said, half-proud and half-embarrassed. "Can you look after Garrik for me during our date?"

"With pleasure." The Tyrant nodded, exchanging a quick look with the bartender. "My children love their uncle, and we’ll protect him against anything and anyone. Isn’t that right, kids?"

Orikan and Jirya had followed their father with their tiny wings and were now clinging to his shoulders with their claws. They answered his question by opening their mouths and showing a row of shark-like teeth that spun at high speed like a circular buzzsaw.

"I didn’t know Tyrants could do that." Haug said in shock.

"Me neither." Morok scratched his chin. "Guys, we have a problem."

"Wah?" The twins looked at their father, waiting to be praised.

"Don’t you do that again unless it’s absolutely necessary. Especially around your mother when she’s about to breastfeed you, or you’ll give her a heart attack. Okay?" He asked, and the children nodded. "Good kids. Now let’s go. I need to find a way to break the news to Quylla."

"Why do you need to tell her about my date?" Ryla blushed.

"I was talking about the teeth thing." The Tyrant replied.

"Oh. Sorry about that." She cleared her throat in embarrassment.

"Don’t worry, Ryla. Just have fun."
