"Here I am, Verhen." Baryl appeared on the parapet, her face a mask of focus and determination.

Her skin was taut and covered in sweat from the effort of suppressing her Upyr side and hiding Light Soul’s flame. Lith used every means at his disposal to check on her life force, but from that distance, the magical formation clouded his mystical senses.

"I appreciate your offer, Red Mother, but I don’t need to be rescued." Baryl said, and the Crone realized the woman truly believed it. "As for you, Verhen, you have my pity. Going so far as to chase groundless rumors to keep the power of your bloodline for yourself.

The ground beneath her feet trembled and cracked, and the spring breeze first turned into a strong wind and then into a tornado that descended from the sky onto the Galnorn Household.

Rivers of magma gushed from the depths of Mogar while every shadow turned into a pool of poison and every sunbeam into a sword of light.

’By my Mom!’ Solus had a hard time believing the reading of the Eyes. ’Malyshka isn’t controlling the power of the elements contained in the world energy. She’s controlling the elements that comprise Mogar itself!

Baba Yaga’s White Core spell, Calamity End, tore the Galnorn Household’s magical defenses apart like paper and reached the grey stone fortress in seconds. The spell conjured localized natural disasters that no magic Lith knew could resist.

It was akin to fighting the planet itself, and no human, beast, or plant folk could ever be up to the challenge. The fortress collapsed like a house of cards when the Crone raised her hand, and the power of the elements crushed what was left in a vise when she closed her fist.

"Cilgan." Her voice snapped the young man out of his terrified daze, making him flinch. "You’re all that’s left of your bloodline, so I made sure not to destroy the treasure vault. You are going to need it to rebuild your Household, but first you need to move it to safety.

"Be quick, because once Lith and I leave, I expect lots of scavengers to rush here and try to rob you blind. Only the strong can own riches. If you hold more than you can protect, you’re just begging to be robbed."

As she spoke, Lith used his enhanced sense of smell to spot the scent of Upyr blood amid the piles of debris and locate what was left of Baryl’s corpse.

’I’m sorry, kid. I really am. Neither of your parents deserved you.’ A burst of Dread Flames erased every trace of the corpses, leaving nothing behind that might have been studied and used to replicate the monstrosity that the Vurdalak was.

Before returning home, Lith visited Althika in the Desert to report to her the fate of her fellow members of Orpal’s harem.
