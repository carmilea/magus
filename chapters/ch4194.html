All the atrophied mana organs inside her body had come back to life, but she had no idea how to activate them. Her entire being burned with power like the day she had become a white core, but this time she knew why.

Not only was Silverwing now aware of the existence of her apex form, but the Demon Dirge also pushed her life force in the right direction.

The space on her forehead and cheeks where her extra eyes were supposed to appear itched, while small bone protuberances bulged on her shoulder blades.

’I know what I’m supposed to look like. I can more or less guess my powers, but I have no idea how to cross the divide that separates my human from my apex form.’ She had thought a few seconds ago as she bolted at top speed towards the Mansion.

Silverwing had already missed Lith’s awakening of the humans in Jiera and didn’t want to miss this opportunity as well. She knew that the effect was bound to decrease with distance, so she used a mix of sonic flight and Warp Steps to reach Lutia.

Unlike Vastor, however, she had to do everything by herself and lacked Tezka’s mastery over space and Chaos. When the Demon Dirge ended, she was still far from the mansion, and she had collected only a few scans of her life force during the travel.
